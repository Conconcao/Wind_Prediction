# Paper List for Xinyang 15-Min Wind Speed Forecasting

## Review papers

1. Alves et al. (2023)
   - Title: The Potential of Machine Learning for Wind Speed and Direction
     Short-Term Forecasting: A Systematic Review
   - Link: <https://www.mdpi.com/2073-431X/12/10/206>

2. Wang et al. (2021)
   - Title: A review of wind speed and wind power forecasting with deep neural
     networks
   - Link: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2021.117766>

3. Abdoos et al. (2021)
   - Title: A review and taxonomy of wind and solar energy forecasting methods
     based on deep learning
   - Link: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.egyai.2021.100060>

## Recurrent and classical benchmark papers

4. Neshat et al. (2020)
   - Title: A new short-term wind speed forecasting method based on fine-tuned
     LSTM neural network and optimal input sets
   - Link: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0196890420303629>

5. Wang et al. (2022)
   - Title: Usage of correlation analysis and hypothesis test in optimizing the
     gated recurrent unit network for wind speed forecasting
   - Link: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2021.122960>

6. Liu et al. (2021)
   - Title: Short-term offshore wind speed forecast by seasonal ARIMA - a
     comparison against GRU and LSTM
   - Link: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2021.120492>

8. Wang et al. (2024)

   - Link: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2024.123477>

9. Cai and Li (2024)
   - Title: Short-term wind speed forecast based on dynamic spatio-temporal

   - Link: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2024.124124>
